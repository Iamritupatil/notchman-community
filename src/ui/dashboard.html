        What happens when I use up the Notchman voice?

        Nothing stops. The standard voice keeps reading every TL;DR, and your Notchman voice comes back at the start of next month.

        Can I cancel anytime?

        Yes. Cancel from the link in your receipt email. You keep Pro until the end of the month you paid for, and you're never charged again.

        Do I need an API key or an account?

        No. Pro works the moment you pay: your plan is linked to this computer, and Notchman's cloud makes the TL;DR and the voice.
